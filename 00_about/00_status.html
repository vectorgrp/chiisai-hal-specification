Specification Status

Important

The Chiisai HAL specification set as a whole carries a single lifecycle status. Its current status is released. Individual requirements do not carry their own status — this set-level status applies to all of them.

Lifecycle statuses

Status

Meaning

draft

Under active development; requirements may change without notice.

approved

Reviewed and accepted; changes are controlled.

released

Published for implementation; stability guarantees apply.
